Our Completed Projects

Agricultural Solar Station in El Maghra

A 25 kW ground-mounted station built on concrete foundations in the desert of El Maghra, supplying the farm with clean, reliable power. The panel arrays are set at a carefully studied tilt for maximum year-round output.

Capacity: 25 kW

Solar Water Pump Station for a Farm in Ras Sudr

A ground-mounted solar station running a 10 HP water pump on a farm along the Sharm El Sheikh road in Ras Sudr, using free energy from the sun to cut irrigation running costs.

Capacity: 10 HP

60 kW Agricultural Solar Station in El Wahat

One of our largest projects: a 60 kW ground-mounted station in El Wahat, installed by the Alkyan team on concrete foundations built for harsh desert conditions to deliver clean energy for farming.

Capacity: 60 kW
